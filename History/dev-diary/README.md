# Dev Diary

Chronological, **non-normative** accounts of development work, one entry per
piece of work, filed as `History/dev-diary/<year>/YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`.

Entries record what happened and what was learned at the time they were
written. They are not documentation: current behavior and policy come from the
root policy files, `Docs/`, the code, and the tests. Entries are not silently
rewritten — corrections get a dated addendum at the bottom of the entry.

The Dev Diary obligation itself is policy: every non-trivial change ships a
diary entry in the same change (`CONTRIBUTING.md`, `REPOSITORY_HYGIENE.md`).
